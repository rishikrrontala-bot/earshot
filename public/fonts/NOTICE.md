- **Archivo** — Omnibus-Type. https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo
  Licence: https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo/blob/master/OFL.txt
- **JetBrains Mono** — JetBrains. https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono
  Licence: https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono/blob/master/OFL.txt

They are self-hosted rather than loaded from Google Fonts because the interface
tells the user that nothing leaves their device, and that has to be true in the
network tab as well as in the microphone path.
